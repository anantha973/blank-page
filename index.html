02 / Approach

A method for
looking closer.

The aim is not to collect interface complaints. It is to connect a real user goal to an observed breakdown, explain its impact, and recommend a change that addresses the underlying cause.

01
Understand the context

Product, users, constraints and relevant scenarios.

02
Walk the journey

Follow realistic tasks and capture the interaction.

03
Identify the heuristic

Connect observed behaviour to a usability principle.

04
Prioritise and recommend

Explain severity, root cause and practical next steps.
